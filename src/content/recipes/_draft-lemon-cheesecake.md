instructions:
  - text: "Preheat oven to 160°C/140°C fan-forced. Grease a 6cm-deep, 20cm round springform pan. Process 250g plain sweet biscuits until finely chopped. Add 150g melted butter. Process until combined. Press mixture over base of a prepared pan. Refrigerate for 15 minutes."
    timerMinutes: 15
  - text: "Meanwhile, beat 500g softened cream cheese, 1/2 cup of caster sugar and 3 tsp finely grated lemon rind together until smooth. One at a time add 3 eggs, beating until combined. Add 1/4 lemon juice. Beat for 1 minute. Pour mixture into prepared pan."
  - text: "Bake for 50 minutes to 1 hour or until filling is just set. Cool in oven with door slightly ajar. Refrigerate for 4 hours or until cold."
    timerMinutes: 50
notes: "Can mix gelatin with fresh passionfruit and leave to set on top. The best approach is a low and slow cook to prevent cracking."
