instructions:
  - text: "Preheat the oven to 170°C fan bake. Line a 24 x 13 cm loaf tin with baking paper."
  - text: "Melt together 150g of butter and 100ml of golden syrup in the microwave and stir to combine."
  - text: "In a large bowl sift or whisk together 2 cups of plain flour, 1 tsp of baking powder, 1 tsp of baking soda, 1 tsp of cinnamon, 1 Tbsp of ground ginger, 1/2 tsp of salt and 1 cup of brown sugar."
  - text: "Make a well in the centre and pour in the butter and golden syrup mixture, 1 egg and 1 cup of milk. Stir everything together until well combined."
  - text: "Pour the mixture into the lined loaf tin and crumble over 1 Tbsp of brown sugar."
  - text: "Bake for 1 hour or until a skewer inserted into the middle comes out clean."
